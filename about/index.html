Project

“共趣小斋” 智慧宿舍分配系统      项目负责人

基于2D虚拟人语音驱动算法的研究与应用      项目负责人

【Problem Statement】

A virtual digital human refers to an entity that exists in the non-physical world, created and utilized through computational means, and possesses multiple human-like characteristics (appearance features, human performance abilities, interaction capabilities, etc.). This problem primarily focuses on the 2D animation of real-world characters, specifically learning from videos of real individuals and using voice input to drive the character’s lip movements, facial expressions, and body actions.

A excellent solution can achieve the video of the character completing the new audio broadcast when the user provides the captured video and voice of the character speaking to obtain the new timbre and content of the audio. At the same time, the mouth motion of the person in this video should be based on being able to align the speech content.

【Additional Supplement】

In the output video driven by virtual human, the face of the character needs to be as clear as possible, and the eyes can blink naturally and randomly. At the same time, the teeth of the virtual human are not exactly the same as those in the original character video, but they should be clear and complete, and there should be no problems such as blurred teeth, multiple rows of teeth, and incomplete teeth.
